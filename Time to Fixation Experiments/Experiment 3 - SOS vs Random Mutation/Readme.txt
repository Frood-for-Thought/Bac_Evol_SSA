In this experiment, the folder "Normal SOS Model" has the standard SOS mutation rate as given in "The Goldilocks Principle and Antibiotic Resistance in Bacteria".

The folder "Spontaneous Mutation Model" has a non-SOS mutation rate.

Bacteria are placed on the left hand side of the model at position 16, (not position 50), everything else is preserved.

The time is recorded to test how long it takes a mutant bacteria to fix into a deme.
